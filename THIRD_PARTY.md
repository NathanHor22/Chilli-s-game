The MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite task bundle is downloaded during explicit setup from Google's versioned model hosting:

`https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/pose_landmarker/pose_landmarker_lite/float16/1/pose_landmarker_lite.task`

The bundle contains the pose detector and pose landmark models. See the [official model documentation](https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/pose_landmarker) and its linked model card for model details. The downloader verifies its pinned SHA-256, size, archive integrity, and the expected TensorFlow Lite files before installation. The running game does not fetch models.

Model SHA-256: `59929e1d1ee95287735ddd833b19cf4ac46d29bc7afddbbf6753c459690d574a`.

The packaging specification retains installed package metadata, including the license files supplied in it, for the direct and transitive dependencies. It also copies the build interpreter's license to `_internal/licenses/python` and the full LGPL 2.1 text supplied by Pygame to `_internal/licenses/pygame`. The source copy is [licenses/pygame-LGPL-2.1.txt](licenses/pygame-LGPL-2.1.txt), retrieved unmodified from the [Pygame 2.6.1 source](https://github.com/pygame/pygame/blob/2.6.1/docs/LGPL.txt). Additional native libraries may be included by the dependency and packaging tools. Keep the entire portable folder when distributing the game.

The chilli artwork and game sound effects are generated by the application code. No images from the supplied installation-reference documents are redistributed as game assets. Chili's names and marks remain those of their respective owners.
